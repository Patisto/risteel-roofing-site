About R & I Steel

Building Malawi’s homes and businesses, one roof at a time.

Our story

A local team built on practical skill and honest work.

R & I Steel Roofing is a provider of steel roofing and fabrication solutions in Malawi. Our team works on steel roof trusses, iron sheet roofing, custom steel windows, electrical installations and plumbing.

From the first site measure through to handover, we aim to communicate clearly, specify materials up front and deliver careful workmanship.
