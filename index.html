Ask us a question ↗
What services does R & I Steel Roofing provide?

Steel truss installation, iron sheet installation, steel window fabrication, electrical installation and plumbing for residential and commercial projects.

Do you work on homes and commercial buildings?

Yes. Contact us with your project details so we can discuss the scope and requirements.

Can you install steel trusses and iron sheet roofing together?

Yes. We can discuss both parts of your roof project together.

Can you fabricate custom steel windows?

Yes. We make custom steel window frames to suit your dimensions, security needs and design.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or WhatsApp +265 889 397 498, or email info@risteelroofing.com. We can arrange a site visit, take measurements and prepare a quote.

Where are you based?

Chichiri, Blantyre, Malawi. We serve homes and businesses across Malawi.
